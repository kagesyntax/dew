# Changelog

All notable changes to this app. The release workflow copies the section for each version into
`version.json`, which powers Settings → Software Update and the What's New sheet.
Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com), versions follow [SemVer](https://semver.org).

## [Unreleased]

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-14
### Added
- The full Sunday School manual is now in Dew: 24 lessons from 5 July to 27 December 2026, covering Units 1-6 plus the Christmas and Special Edition studies
- Unit 1 "Unsung Heroes": Unselfish Heroes, Desperate Heroes, A Truthful Hero, A Courageous Hero
- Unit 2 "Miracles of Jesus": The Reality of Miracles, The Reasons For Miracles, The Responses To Miracles
- Unit 3 "Countering the Culture": A Different World, Getting The Message, When People Give In To Peer Pressure
- Unit 4 "Ancient Faith/Future Faith": Finding Your Centre, Understanding Your Saviour, Keeping Your Faith
- Lesson content corrected against the printed manual: the 1 November discussion, Act and Daily Dew Drops had been carried over from a different study
### Fixed
- The 1 November lesson now shows its own questions (No Profit, No Comparison, No Turning Back) instead of the wrong study's material
- The 1 November Daily Dew Drops, Act, memory verse and page numbers now match the printed manual

## [0.1.3] - 2026-10-14
### Changed
- Dew now fills the whole screen on Android and iPhone: the status bar and navigation area match your theme.

## [0.1.2] - 2026-10-14
### Fixed
- The Android app now installs on real phones, including older 32-bit ones, and shows the Dew icon
- A separate download for Intel Macs (2020 and older), running macOS 10.15 or newer

## [0.1.1] - 2026-10-14
### Added
- Reader toolbar above the tab bar: previous and next chapter, tap the passage to jump to any book and chapter, switch translation. It tucks away while you read and returns when you scroll up
- Arrow keys turn chapters on a keyboard
- Verse button in the reader toolbar: follows where you are reading, and lists every verse with its opening words; type a number or words to find one (Psalm 119 grouped by Hebrew letter)
- Verse scrubber for long chapters as an alternative (Settings → Find a Verse)
- Reader toolbar redesigned: docked on the tab bar and always visible
- Settings, Bible, Open Where I Left Off: the Bible tab reopens your chapter and the verse you searched for, or starts at the list of books
### Fixed
- Inline verses now open full width under the paragraph everywhere in a lesson, with long passages folded behind Show All
- The translation picker opened off screen from the reader's top bar
- The reader showed the translation twice; it now lives only at the top right

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-14
### Added
- Bible reader with King James, World English, Berean Standard and American Standard versions, fully offline
- Sunday School manual with memory verse, discussion questions, leader notes and Daily Dew Drops
- Tap any reference in a lesson to preview it, expand it inline, or jump to the Bible and back
- Highlights in four colours, bookmarks, copy and share
- Welcome tour and in-context tips
- Light and dark appearance, text size, and verse-number options
- Software Update in Settings, delivered from GitHub releases
- Apps for Android, Windows, Mac and Linux, plus the web
- The Dew website, where you can try the app before downloading it
